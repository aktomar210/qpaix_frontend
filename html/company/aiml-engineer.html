FULL-TIME

AI/ML Engineer

Ahmedabad, Gujarat

This position is no longer accepting applications.

QPAIX Infitech is hiring an AI/ML Engineer to build practical, production-grade machine learning features for our enterprise clients. You'll work across our product suite — from predictive maintenance in CMMS to intelligent automation in our ERP/HRMS platform, Gyan Saarthi — turning real business problems into deployed ML solutions, not just research prototypes.

What You'll Do

  • Design and build ML pipelines for process automation, predictive analytics, and NLP-driven features integrated into existing QPAIX products
  • Take models from experimentation to production — training, evaluation, deployment, and monitoring
  • Work with structured enterprise data (ERP, HRMS, maintenance logs) to build models that solve concrete operational problems
  • Collaborate with backend engineers to expose ML capabilities via clean, well-documented APIs
  • Continuously evaluate and improve model performance based on real production feedback

What We're Looking For

  • 2+ years of experience building and deploying ML models in a real product environment (not only academic/research projects)
  • Strong Python skills with experience in scikit-learn, PyTorch, or TensorFlow
  • Working knowledge of NLP techniques and/or time-series/predictive analytics
  • Comfort with SQL and working directly with enterprise datasets
  • Bonus: experience with LLM-based features, vector databases, or MLOps tooling

Why Join QPAIX

  • Your models ship into real products used by enterprise clients — not shelved after a demo
  • Direct collaboration with the product and engineering teams shaping QPAIX's AI roadmap
  • Based in Ahmedabad, with room to define how AI gets used across the whole product suite
